      About EVENTORA

      Discover Events. Create Experiences. Make Memories.

      EVENTORA is an event discovery and management platform designed to bring people and experiences together.

      Explore exciting events, discover experiences based on your interests, and register for events through a simple and seamless process.

      EVENTORA also empowers event organizers to submit and manage their events, while providing administrators with tools to review submissions and manage the event ecosystem.

      Key highlights

      • Discover events across different categories.
      • Search and explore upcoming experiences.
      • Register for events easily.
      • Receive digital registration tickets with QR codes.
      • Manage event submissions and approvals.
      • Simplify event organization and attendance management.
